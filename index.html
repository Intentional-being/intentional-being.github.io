Ankia Admin

A private, single-user automation tool. Last updated 7 October 2026.

What this app is

Ankia Admin is a personal automation agent operated by, and used exclusively for, one Google account holder. It runs on a private server owned by that account holder. It is not offered to the public, has no other users, and is not a commercial product or service.

What it does

  • Mail triage. Once a day it reads the unread mail in the account holder's own Gmail inbox, classifies it by sender into labelled lanes (receipts, banking, research feeds, notifications, and similar), applies the corresponding Gmail labels, and removes the messages from the inbox. It never deletes messages, and never touches starred mail, drafts or spam.
  • Daily digest. It sends the account holder a short summary of what was filed, what needs action, and any research items of interest.
  • Filter upkeep. It creates and maintains Gmail filters that route recurring senders into those labels automatically.
  • Research and calendar. It reads public regulatory and market feeds to produce briefings, and reads and writes events on the account holder's own Google Calendar.

Google user data

The app requests access to the following Google APIs, for the account holder's own data only, and only for the functions described above:

  • Gmail — read messages, apply labels, create filters, send mail on the account holder's behalf
  • Google Calendar — read and create events
  • Google Drive, Docs and Sheets — read and write the account holder's own documents and spreadsheets
  • Contacts (read-only) — distinguish mail from known contacts

No Google user data is transmitted to any third party, sold, used for advertising or profiling, or used to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes. No human other than the account holder reads it. Processed data and OAuth credentials are stored only on the account holder's own server.
